Around the hen

Turn, step, forage.

Eight viewpoints of the same hen. She watches with her head up, holds and advances her head as she walks, and lowers once for a bout of two to four pecks before raising her head.

The body holds its shape while the neck, wings and legs articulate. These are the same movements used in the painting.
