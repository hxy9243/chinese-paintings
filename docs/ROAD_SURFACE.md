## Evidence and scope

The primary excavation report, [河南开封北宋东京城顺天门遗址2012—2017年勘探发掘简报](https://m.hnswwkgyjy.cn/ueditor/php/upload/file/20220524/1653363795965934.pdf), printed page 25, describes Song roads L39 and L32 inside Shuntian Gate. The road soil is hard and smooth: L39 reddish brown, L32 grey-white. Both preserve parallel wheel tracks with centres 1.10 m apart; recorded depths are approximately 2–5 cm or 3–5 cm depending on the track. The original PDF text was retrieved and checked locally during this revision.

This is direct evidence for earthen road surfaces elsewhere in Bianliang. Applying similar wear at Rainbow Bridge is an informed interpretation, not site-specific archaeological proof. Neither the city's importance nor masonry water channels establishes continuous stone paving on these approaches. The supplied painting reads as continuous ground without a clear paving grid, but the painting alone cannot establish its material.

The user's preference for one ground palette is retained. There is no contrasting road overlay, universal stone pavement, or unsupported claim about the bridge deck's exact historical surfacing.
