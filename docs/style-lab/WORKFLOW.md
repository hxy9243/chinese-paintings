# Draft workflow: reference-led Chinese painting modeling and rendering

Use for a new controlled Chinese painting study or review of an existing reconstruction. This is an uninstalled workflow draft, not a claim of historical authenticity or a production-ready skill. Keep the current project baseline intact.

1. **Freeze evidence and scope.** Record repository commit, dirty state, reference file hashes, crop pixel bounds, attribution and uncertainty. Use actual pixels, not a remembered painting. Separate geometry fidelity, style plausibility and engineering gates. Select a small building/tree/bank-water patch. Freeze its geometry, camera, seed, resolution, time and LOD for every style comparison.
2. **Register before decorating.** Mark roof ridge endpoints, eaves, tree roots/forks, shoreline corners and scaffold outer silhouette in reference coordinates. Record occlusion order and negative spaces. Fit a camera using several anchors; hold others out for evaluation. Report normalized landmark error and silhouette overlap with residual overlays. A handscroll may use locally varying projection: do not warp geometry to force one global camera match. Unseen elevations are explicitly inferred.
3. **Verify anatomy in neutral materials.** Architecture: distinguish roof type, ridge/valley topology, eave direction, bay rhythm, open/closed facades, post load paths and scaffold connections. Trees: classify visible branching evidence before species; preserve trunk gesture, taper, major forks, pollard heads, crown gaps and root anchoring. Avoid evenly spaced radial branch fans and interchangeable foliage balls. Rocks/banks: preserve strata, erosion notches, bank height, exposed toe and water clearance; do not trace every triangulation edge. Review front, side and reverse views before adding brush effects.
4. **Use object-specific marks.** Fine architecture: restrained color, thin continuous structural lines, quieter tile courses, selective silhouette emphasis. Landscape: directional bark and rock strokes following form, broken dry contours and sparse foliage masses. Expressive ink is interpretive; expose width/pressure, ink value, dryness and moisture separately. Blue-green study: layered pigment regions and rock directions with open water; avoid a global cyan grade. Silk support is not coarse watercolor paper. Sepia and noise alone are not ink technique.
5. **Attach marks and test causality.** Use stable per-object seeds, local coordinates or authored UV/tangent fields; avoid per-frame randomness. Filter high-frequency marks at minification. Compare one mechanism at a time with palette/geometry/camera fixed, such as directional mark opacity on/off. A four-style comparison alone does not isolate causation. Keep global diffusion away from fine architecture/depth boundaries.
6. **Check time and orbit.** Save a fixed-camera repeat after an orbit, adjacent small-angle frames, wide front/side/back views and a short orbit loop. Verify unchanged object hashes and exact replay pixels where deterministic. Inspect roof aliasing, twig dropout, silhouette popping and screen-space shower-door effects. Exact return-frame agreement is necessary but does not prove absence of swimming during motion.
7. **Measure performance and LOD.** Record hardware/backend, viewport/DPR, warmed median/p95 frame time, all render passes' draw calls, triangles, capture latency, cold compile time separately. Preserve nested stroke sets across LOD, add hysteresis, and never replace a distinctive joined roof with a generic gable. Batch compatible geometry before adding detail. Demand-render static frames, invalidate camera/resize/parameter/asset changes and continue frames during damping or animation.
8. **Use honest gates.** Pass only the tested scope. Proposed reference gate: held-out landmark error <=2% crop diagonal and correct occlusion/roof/tree anatomy, reviewed in an overlay; tune thresholds before fitting, not after. Style gate: legible hierarchy, material-specific marks and reference-supported substrate/pigment handling. Temporal gate: no conspicuous crawl/pop in adjacent frames at delivery size. Performance gate: explicit target-device budget, e.g. p95 <=33 ms for a 30 fps target. Record failed, blocked and not-run independently. Build/tests do not certify visual fidelity. Never promote a palette experiment as a reconstruction.
